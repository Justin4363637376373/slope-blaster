The 3 rules

1. Start at b. The line crosses the y-axis at (0, b).
2. Move with m. Slope = rise ÷ run. From b, go right by the bottom number, then up (or down if m is negative) by the top number.
3. Read the sign. m > 0 goes uphill, m < 0 goes downhill, m = 0 is flat.
